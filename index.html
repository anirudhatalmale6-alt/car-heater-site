Good to know before you buy

This is a spot heater and defroster. It is designed to clear a patch of windscreen and warm the area right in front of it - it will not heat the whole car the way your car's own heater does.

Use it with the engine running. A heater on the 12V socket with the engine off will run the battery down.

Questions

Will it work in my car?

If your car has a 12V cigarette-lighter style socket - and almost every car does - it will plug straight in.

Does it heat the whole car?

No. It is a spot heater: it puts warm air where you point it, which is ideal for clearing the windscreen or warming your hands, but it is not a replacement for the car's heating.

Can I use it with the engine off?

Please don't. It draws its power from the car battery, so use it while the engine is running.

Can I use it in summer?

Yes - switch it to cool air and it works as a small fan.
